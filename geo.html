Geospatial Engineering

My background starts in Geospatial Engineering, where I built an end-to-end remote sensing workflow for Nyatike, Migori—fusing Sentinel-1 SAR (for all-weather flood detection) with a Sentinel-2 Random Forest land-cover model across 4,366 hectares (91.8% validation accuracy). Post-graduation, I took those spatial data pipelines into boundary mapping, county health analytics, and mineral studies.

FloodsDegree project

Nyatike, Migori

The rivers Kuja and Migori break their banks, and a ground count of every household is too slow for the people who have to respond.

I mapped the water with Sentinel-1, classified the land cover with Sentinel-2, and subtracted one from the other so the damaged classes were visible. Earth Engine ran the processing. The report is the University of Nairobi final project, May 2024. I can walk through it.

Sentinel-1 · Sentinel-2 · Google Earth Engine · Random Forest

BoundariesPublic

RealitySync

Kenya’s administrative boundaries, from the country down through the county to the sub-county, need someone willing to say a polygon is wrong before it is used to train a model.

RealitySync does that check, keeps the curated set, and shows it on a MapLibre map. dbt holds the models on PostGIS. Docker, Terraform, and Cloud Build ship it, with Nginx in front. The paper was accepted as a poster at IEEE AP-GARSS 2026 in Qingdao.

FastAPI · PostGIS · rasterio · TiTiler · dbt · MapLibre · Docker · Terraform · Cloud Build

Mining satellite study

The question was what had changed in that ground between 2001–2006 and 2023–2024.

The box is about 4.8°N–5.1°N and 29.3°E–29.6°E. ASTER picks out clay, iron, and carbonate. PALSAR picks out lineaments. SRTM sits underneath so the geology has a ground. A third notebook puts the two periods together.

Google Earth Engine · geemap · ASTER · ALOS PALSAR · SRTM · Jupyter

FloodsDegree project

Nyatike, Migori

Rapid flood extent and damage assessment for Nyatike constituency.

The rivers Kuja and Migori break their banks, and a ground count of every household is too slow for the people who have to respond. I mapped the water with Sentinel-1, classified the land cover with Sentinel-2, and subtracted one from the other so the damaged classes were visible. Earth Engine ran the processing. The report is the University of Nairobi final project, May 2024. I can walk through it.

Sentinel-1 · Sentinel-2 · Google Earth Engine · Random Forest

RealitySync

A place to check a boundary before you trust it.

Kenya’s administrative boundaries, from the country down through the county to the sub-county, need a person or a program willing to say a polygon is wrong before it is used to train a model. RealitySync does that check, keeps the curated set, and shows it on a MapLibre map. dbt holds the models on PostGIS. Docker, Terraform, and Cloud Build ship it, with Nginx in front. The paper was accepted as a poster at IEEE AP-GARSS 2026 in Qingdao.

FastAPI · PostGIS · rasterio · TiTiler · dbt · MapLibre · Docker · Terraform · Cloud Build

Mining satellite study

One study area, looked at twice, twenty years apart.

The box is about 4.8°N–5.1°N and 29.3°E–29.6°E. ASTER picks out clay, iron, and carbonate. PALSAR picks out lineaments. Both periods are 2001–2006 and 2023–2024, with SRTM underneath so the geology has a ground. A third notebook puts the two together.

Google Earth Engine · geemap · ASTER · ALOS PALSAR · SRTM · Jupyter
